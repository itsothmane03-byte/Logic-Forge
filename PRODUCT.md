# Fallacy Forge

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People practising reasoning in short sessions, including beginners. Examples
should test reasoning without requiring specialist background knowledge.

## Product Purpose

Help people examine everyday arguments, identify weaknesses, choose stronger
revisions, and distinguish what follows from given facts.

## Operating Context

The shared Daily Forge has ten questions per local calendar date. Separate
practice offers adaptive fallacy exercises, argument repair, and deduction.
Interests are optional soft preferences for personal practice, not a filter on
the shared daily set. The library and progress pages support deeper study.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Static Preact and TypeScript PWA. Reviewed, fictional content: 304 fallacy
questions, 38 fallacies, 15 repair exercises, and 18 deduction puzzles. No
accounts, analytics, backend, or runtime AI. Browser progress, backups, scores,
unfinished drafts, themes, keyboard controls, and existing routes must survive
visual changes. Do not reveal answer clues before checking.

## Brand Commitments

Fallacy Forge name and orange eye mark. Plain, concise language. Free access.
No music. No personal creator credit in the interface. The owner has delegated
design judgment and implementation to Codex coordinating with Claude; the
2026-10-25 request explicitly expands the earlier polish-only brief to a
distinctive UI and clearer workflow. Avoid a paid-app onboarding funnel.
The owner explicitly prioritises avoiding a playful, game-like feel.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing reviewed exercise content, working activities, repository tests, and
screenshots in docs/screenshots. Three owner-supplied design-tool reels have
local transcripts and setup evidence in the separate Codex setup workspace.
No testimonials, market-performance evidence, or supplied Figma design file.

## Product Principles

- Let people try reasoning before explaining the entire product.
- Distinguish the shared daily challenge from optional personal practice.
- Reveal detail as it becomes useful; keep preferences optional.
- Preserve accurate explanations and saved progress over visual novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, reduced motion, readable contrast,
and responsive layouts in light and dark themes. Verify at 320px and equivalent
zoom reflow. Automated checks do not certify assistive-technology support.
